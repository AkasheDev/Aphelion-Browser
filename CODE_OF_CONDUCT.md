# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone involved in Aphelion.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and respectful in all interactions.
- Welcome different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, spam, or disruptive behavior.
- Sharing private information without permission.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainers.
